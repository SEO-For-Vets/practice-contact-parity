Example Veterinary Practice: new-client website plan

A reception-led brief based on the practice arrangements you supplied.

Who can register
- Animal groups: Dogs, Cats, Small pets
- Action: Call reception about registration. Show the actual reception number and opening arrangements.

Routine appointments
- Action: Request a routine appointment. State clearly that the practice must confirm the appointment.
- Keep urgent and out-of-hours information clearly separate from routine booking.

Help clients choose a branch
- Choose the appropriate branch and check its actual address, opening hours and access information.

Urgent and out-of-hours information
- No approved wording supplied. Ask the responsible practice team to provide the actual urgent and out-of-hours instructions.
- Do not publish a guessed emergency number or let a routine enquiry form appear to be an urgent-care route.

Before the first appointment
- Reception explains registration and how previous records are requested where appropriate.

Reception-team review
- Confirm all contact details, branch routing, registration and booking arrangements.
- Check the journey on a phone and arrange a controlled test of enquiry delivery.
- Do not promise clinical outcomes, availability or registration acceptance that the practice has not confirmed.
